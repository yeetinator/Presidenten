# Sound credits

The card sounds (`card-*.mp3`) are from **Casino Audio 1.1** by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), converted from `.ogg` to `.mp3`.

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The tonal cues (jump-in alert, pass knock, quad slam, player out, round end) are synthesized in `src/lib/audio.ts`.
